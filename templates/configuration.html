Sample overlays

          
        

        
A DSE → outlet workbook is read in the browser
          on the Preview Polygon page and drawn
          over the permanent layers. Nothing is uploaded to preview one, which
          is what makes it safe on a shared host and why a 2.5 MB file appears
          in about a quarter of a second.

        
Anything kept on this server is visible to
          everyone who opens that page. Removing one asks for a PIN — a guard
          against a careless click, not access control.
