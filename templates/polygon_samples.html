The columns a workbook needs are a DSE code, a longitude and a latitude; DSE CODE or UNIKDSE, LONG or LONGITUDE, LAT or LATITUDE are all accepted. Every sheet is tried and the one carrying those three wins, so a workbook with Summary and Maps tabs does not need cleaning up first. Rows without a DSE code, without coordinates, or outside {{ home_name }} are counted and named rather than dropped in silence.
