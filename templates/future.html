2 Population

3 Distance to the existing estate

Not in the document. §0 spends distance as a hard gate; a 10 km cut removes all twenty of the largest kecamatan, which all sit 2–6 km from a counter. Runs under this setting are labelled a local variant.

The document's six terms are renormalised to (1 − weight), so they keep their ratios to each other.
